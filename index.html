Our Client Process

From initial consultation to white-glove handover.

We manage every phase in-house — ensuring precision measurements, transparent costing, and flawless installation without project delays.

01

3D Site Survey & Spatial Planning

We visit your property to assess architectural sightlines, plumbing/electrical runs, and conduct precision laser measurements.

02

Material Curation & Detailed Quote

You select your finish band and hardware; we provide complete 3D elevations and an itemized, transparent quotation.

03

Precision Workshop Fabrication

Your cabinetry is custom manufactured in our Industrial Area workshop with automated edge-banding and strict QA inspections.

04

White-Glove On-Site Installation

Our master installers fit the units, align Blum hardware, seal all surfaces, and complete a final snag-free inspection.
