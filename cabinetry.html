02 / Cabinetry

Choose your
cabinetry finish.

Five clearly defined finish bands help clients compare the look, material and level of finish that suits their design, use and budget. Final specifications are confirmed during consultation.

ℹ

No fixed prices are shown. Final costs depend on scope, measurements, hardware and selected materials. All pricing is confirmed after your consultation and site visit.

Cabinetry finish bands

MELAMINE
VERSATILE · PRACTICAL
Band 01 — Melamine
01

Melamine-faced boards

Melamine-faced particleboard or MDF

A practical, versatile option with a wide range of colours and woodgrain finishes. Ideal for everyday kitchens, rentals and budget-conscious projects that still demand a clean, finished look.

Ideal for Rentals, apartments, functional kitchens
Request Quote
GLOSS / UV
SLEEK · CONTEMPORARY
Band 02 — Gloss / UV
02

UV glazed & gloss finishes

UV glazed, high-gloss or equivalent boards

A sleek contemporary look with reflective or polished surfaces. Subject to selected board availability, this finish delivers a high-impact visual presence with low maintenance.

Ideal for Modern kitchens, feature bathrooms
Request Quote
SHAKER & FLAT
CLASSIC · FLEXIBLE
Band 03 — Shaker & Flat Panel
03

Shaker-style & flat panels

Shaker-style wood, spray-painted flat panels and PVC wraps

A flexible style band for classic shaker details or clean flat-front designs. Spray-painted finishes and PVC wraps offer a refined, tailored appearance across a range of colour choices.

Ideal for Classic kitchens, family homes, developer units
Request Quote
RAISED MDF
DECORATIVE · TAILORED
Band 04 — Raised MDF
04

MDF raised panels

MDF raised panels with spray paint

A more traditional decorative profile with a painted finish and a tailored colour choice. The raised panel detail adds depth and visual interest, suited to formal rooms and statement kitchens.

Ideal for Formal kitchens, dining rooms, statement spaces
Request Quote
Band 05 — Solid Wood
05

Mvule, mahogany or Meru oak

Natural solid timber — selected to specification

Natural timber character for clients seeking distinctive grain, warmth and a premium solid-wood finish. Each piece is crafted from carefully selected mvule, mahogany or Meru oak to your specification.

Ideal for Premium kitchens, statement wardrobes, heritage homes
